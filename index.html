Helix Mono SemiBold, 84/86/−55

Helix Mono is a monospace typeface for code, data and the interfaces built around them. It starts from JetBrains Mono, an open source face drawn for long hours in editors, and changes it only where a change earns its place. The point of every V notch is rounded off by a small circle that touches both edges, so a, b, d, g, h, m, n, p, q and r keep the same soft joint in every weight. Heavier weights get rounder notches, because their strokes are thicker. The r ends square, with no lip, and its arm eases down toward its end in one curve. The u is a turned n.

The g's bottom hook is its bottom-right corner mirrored, stroke for stroke, and it stays clear of the bowl. The y's foot stops below its crotch. The M is exactly symmetric, and the inner edges of its uprights run straight up its grooves. The Q's tail stays clear of the counter. The ampersand and question mark are drawn lighter than the stems, as JetBrains Mono's own are, so they never look darker than the letters around them. The zero keeps its dot, sized so the Bold's never fills its counter. Each italic is its upright slanted nine degrees, so every edit carries over.
